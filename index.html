Sustainability Through the Ages

Explore Intel's journey through time — from innovation milestones to bold sustainability goals that help protect our planet.

Intel Sustainability Timeline

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, sparking a revolution in computing.

1978

8086 Processor

Launch of the 8086 processor, establishing the x86 architecture that powers modern computing.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture, enabling enhanced performance.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel records its highest operational GHG emissions and invests in renewable energy, abatement and efficiency improvements.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches the RISE strategy (Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling) and sets 2030 sustainability goals.

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel commits to net-zero Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions across global operations by 2040.

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide, a major milestone in reducing carbon emissions.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its Sustainability Summit to unite suppliers and leaders on sustainable semiconductor manufacturing.
